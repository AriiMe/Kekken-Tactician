# Tekken 4 archive expansion

Expanded the 18 independent fighter guides with self-contained move commands, matching frame-table entries, grouped juggle follow-ups and separate wall routes. Eddy links to Christie; Combot remains a mimic. Frame values preserve per-hit sequences, crouching-hit differences and knockdown results. Missing or ambiguous values are omitted, not estimated.

Sources:

- [Movelists and linked character tables](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206040806/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/_movelist.php)
- [Frame data and its column definitions](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206040808/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/_framedata.php)
- [Character combo tables](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206040808/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/_combolist.php)
- [System overview](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206040807/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/_system.php), including linked Wall Basics and Wall Damage & Tech Rolls pages
- [Jake_HK's Laser Scraper guide](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206044642/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/faq/gen-lsescape-01.txt)
- [Griever's additional juggles](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206044634/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/faq/gen-combo-02.txt)
- [IAM's recovery traps](https://web.archive.org/web/20201206044632/http://www.tekkenzaibatsu.com/tekken4/faq/gen-combo-01.txt)

Laser Scraper's normal, long-charge and just-frame behavior is explained separately. IAM's three selections are recovery setups, not guaranteed juggles. The roster includes concise position-change, wall recovery and wall-jump instructions. Numeric stagger advantage is not labeled as a guaranteed punish window.

This is selected coverage, not a full transcription. Implicit move-tree branches, unclear whiff notation, unsupported frame annotations and speculative routes were excluded. Row-specific combo restrictions override shared launcher headings, including Lee's Acid Rain exception. Source prose, signatures and claimed damage totals are not copied into guides. Routes are source-checked and input-tested, not independently emulator-tested; the early FAQs describe arcade-era behavior.
